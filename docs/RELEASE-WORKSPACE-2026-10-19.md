# Workspace usability and marketing update

Hiring now starts with position cards grouped by country and city. Location, position-title and active/closed filters organize the view. Counts reflect the filtered positions. Clicking any post opens only that opening's full applicant list and top-three shortlist. All profiles remain available in the cross-opening inbox. The previous automatic selection of the newest opening and the redundant opening switcher were removed.

The primary navigation now exposes Home, Schedule, Customers, Work, Hiring, and Marketing according to permissions. Reports, jobs, estimates, invoices, team and settings remain in More. The Home page leads with four task shortcuts; detailed business performance is expandable. Menus close after navigation, the current primary section has one highlight, and smaller screens use the same mobile menu.

Marketing includes actual CRM source/campaign analysis, request approvals, a tracked-link builder and manually entered USD marketing spend with cost-per-request calculations. Source tags survive navigation from public landing pages to the quote form in the current browser session. No advertising accounts, analytics providers, or paid campaigns were activated. Refer to MARKETING.md for calculation and attribution limits.

The additive sixth database migration creates MarketingSpend. Permissions are limited to owners and managers. Unit/domain verification covers 11 scenarios; isolated database verification covers 16; browser verification covers 12. All passed, including the final targeted usability checks. The production build is also required before pushing this release. The live handybandi.com site and DNS are untouched; deployment remains the existing Railway staging environment.
