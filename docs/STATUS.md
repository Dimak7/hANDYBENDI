## Acceptance scenarios

**Residential:** request → CRM → itemized estimate → customer approval → job → assignment → technician progress → recorded office sign-off → closure is implemented. Photo documentation, automatic final invoicing, payment collection, and review requests remain.

**Multi-service visit:** a request can include many services in one lead and written estimate; job team coverage considers all requested service skills. Per-service cost tracking and final consolidated invoice issuing remain.

**Commercial property manager:** the data hierarchy starts with accounts/properties/buildings/units. Access is isolated at property query level. Work-order submission across five units, NTE rules, PO tracking, completion reports, and consolidated Net-30 billing remain.

**Employee operations:** provisioning, skill records, assignment, and own-job visibility work. Availability/profile editing, time tracking, approvals, compensation, and productivity/cost reports remain.

**Warranty:** closed jobs receive a configurable warranty expiration, and a claim model exists. Eligibility review, linked return-job creation, customer request UI, and separate warranty costs remain.
