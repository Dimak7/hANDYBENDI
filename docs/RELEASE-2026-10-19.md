This is a working staging milestone, not completion of the five-phase business platform. No live HandyBandi site, DNS, customer message, vacancy or paid advertising campaign was changed.

## Admin setup

The first owner was provisioned once with a unique strong password. Owner sign-in was verified against staging. The local ignored `artifacts/staging-access.json` contains the login URL, email and password. No local development/demo credentials were copied to the cloud, and no password is committed. See DEPLOYMENT.md for trusted provisioning and password-reset requirements.

## Schema and completed functionality

Four Prisma migrations are applied locally and in staging: foundation, integrity constraints, hiring, and private quote photos. Business settings and the service catalog were seeded with DEMO_MODE=false. The only fictional cloud business records are explicitly labeled verification records created by the smoke test.

This release adds the editorial homepage, real existing brand/project imagery, mobile navigation and quote CTA, database-backed service directory and individual service pages, residential/commercial audience routes, landlord/property-manager and business-sector pages, about/contact/pricing review/service-area pages, and a before/after portfolio. Known legacy URLs have matching redirects; the full SEO migration remains a separate launch gate. Public routes have metadata and a sitemap; staging remains noindex.

The progressive repair list supports several services, additional tasks, up to three private photos, contact/address/preferences, separate consent, field validation and retained state on back navigation. It persists to the existing CRM. Image decoding and re-encoding remove metadata and reject disguised/unsupported content. Private S3 downloads require staff CRM access.

The existing CRM, estimate approval, scheduling, dispatch safeguards, job lifecycle, customer scoping and manual applicant workspace remain connected to the same authenticated workspace. Financial screens remain read-only; no payment collection is enabled.

## Marketing and account dependencies

The complete marketing workspace is not built. Existing quote UTM fields persist to the CRM; GA4/Search Console/Google Ads/Meta, campaign/content planning, conversion tracking and collected-revenue attribution remain Phase 3 work. No analytics values are fabricated.

Staging uses Cloudflare's official test keys and a visible testing banner. Real bot-protection keys are needed before receiving real customer traffic. Resend sender verification, Stripe, SMS, Google/Meta and hh.kz permissions/credentials are not configured. No mass communications or paid campaigns were launched.

The Railway backup panel reports that creating/scheduling backups requires Pro. No plan upgrade was performed and no automated backup is claimed. The owner can review the plan at https://railway.com/workspace/plans; upgrading is a separate payment decision. After upgrading, enable schedules on the staging Postgres Backups tab and perform an isolated restore drill. Monitoring currently uses Railway logs and database/catalog healthchecks; alert delivery remains unconfigured.
